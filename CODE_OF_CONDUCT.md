# Code of Conduct

PluginMatrix contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces respectful, technically constructive, and safe for participation.

Be considerate, discuss the work rather than the person, accept good-faith correction, and respect privacy and licensing boundaries. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's sensitive data are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently exclude participants when needed to protect the project community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where possible, and free from conflicts of interest.

For conduct concerns, use a private repository-owner contact channel. Until one is documented, request private contact through a minimal public issue without including sensitive details. Security vulnerabilities should follow `SECURITY.md` instead.

This policy is original project text and is intentionally concise for the project's current size.
